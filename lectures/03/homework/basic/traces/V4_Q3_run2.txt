**RLHF** stands for **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback**. 

While this specific term is not formally defined in the course glossary, it refers to a machine learning technique used to align large language models (LLMs) with human preferences, values, and instructions. In RLHF, a reward model is trained on human preference data, and reinforcement learning (such as PPO—Proximal Policy Optimization) is then used to optimize the LLM so it generates more helpful, harmless, and honest responses.

Regarding where this is covered in the course:
* **Pretraining and Fine-Tuning** (including Supervised Fine-Tuning / SFT) are covered in **Lecture 02** (Fine-Tuning and LoRA), where you explore how models are adapted after initial pretraining. 
* However, the advanced alignment process of RLHF goes beyond standard SFT and is not the primary focus of the core sessions (Lectures 01, 02, 03, and 05), which focus more on AI engineering, agent loops, tools, and MCP rather than training foundation models from scratch.

*(Lecture 02)*
