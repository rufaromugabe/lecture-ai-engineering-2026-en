It is not in the glossary. 

However, RLHF stands for **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback**, which is a crucial training methodology used to align large language models with human preferences, values, and instructions. It typically involves three main steps:
1. Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on demonstration data.
2. Training a reward model based on human comparisons of model outputs.
3. Optimizing the language model using Reinforcement Learning (such as PPO) against that reward model.
