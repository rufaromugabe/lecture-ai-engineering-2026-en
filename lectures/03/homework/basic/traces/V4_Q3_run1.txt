**RLHF** stands for **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback**. It is a training technique used to align large language models (LLMs) so that their outputs are more helpful, honest, and harmless, based on human preferences. 

*(Note: RLHF is not currently covered in the core sessions of the AIE2026 pilot course, which focus more on API usage, fine-tuning with LoRA, AI agents, and external tools like MCP.)*
