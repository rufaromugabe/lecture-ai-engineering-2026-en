* **Differences in Evaluation Conditions:** Public benchmark conditions differ across companies (such as variations in tools and compute), meaning their rankings cannot simply be compared directly or guarantee performance on your specific use case [Lecture 2, p. 39].
* **Data Contamination ("Cheating"):** A model may have seen the test questions during its training phase, which acts as a form of cheating and artificially inflates its benchmark score [Lecture 2, p. 39].
