In the AIE2026 course, the three levels of homework are:

1. **Basic**: Review the lecture content.
2. **Implementation**: Write your own code.
3. **Advanced**: Research and experimentation.

Completing any *one* of these levels is enough to finish the homework for a given lecture, and as a rule, the homework is not graded. 

When you are deciding which level to tackle, think about your current goals and how much time you want to spend experimenting:
- If you want to solidify your understanding of the core concepts covered in the session, starting with **Basic** is a great way to review.
- If you want hands-on practice building or modifying code, try the **Implementation** level.
- If you are feeling curious and want to explore further or test out extra tools and frameworks, give the **Advanced** level a try!
